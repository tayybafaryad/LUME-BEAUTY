Our Story

Slow beauty, gently made

Founded 2019

A ritual, not a routine

LUMÉ began in a small kitchen with a single rosewater cleanser and a belief that skincare should feel like a quiet, unhurried moment in your day — not one more task on the list.

Every formula is small-batch, dermatologist tested, cruelty-free, and built around botanicals that actually do something: rosewater to calm, hyaluronic acid to plump, lilac stem cells to brighten. No fillers, no fuss.
